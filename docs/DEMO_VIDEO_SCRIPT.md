## 🎬 Recording Setup & Production Checklist

### 1. Browser & Display Preparation:
* **Resolution:** Set browser window to exactly **1920x1080 (1080p, 16:9)**.
* **Zoom Level:** Set browser zoom to **90% or 100%** so all dashboard metrics, tables, and buttons fit cleanly without excessive scrolling.
* **Clean Environment:** Hide bookmarks bar, close extraneous tabs, disable desktop notifications.
* **URL to Record:** Record the live production deployment: `https://aegisgov-ai-fnk7rtrpba-as.a.run.app`.

### 2. Tab Navigation Flow to Rehearse:
```
1. Start: Financial Sentinel (Active: Vikram Mehta / Senior Wealth Advisor)
   └── Click: "Direct Prompt Injection & Jailbreak Attack" ──► Show Ingress Interception (BLOCKED_ATTACK)
   └── Click: "Commercial Credit & Lease Payout" ───────────► Show Circuit Breaker (PENDING_HITL_APPROVAL)
2. Switch Persona to: Kenji Sato (Director of Model & Financial Risk)
   └── Open: HITL Approval Center ─────────────────────────► Enter remarks ──► Approve Settlement
3. Navigate: Medallion Lakehouse
   └── Click: 1. Bronze (Raw Lake) ─────────────────────────► Show PoLP HTTP 403 Forbidden Barrier
   └── Click: 2. Silver & 3. Quarantine ────────────────────► Show PII masking & Safeguarded Capital
   └── Click: 4. Gold (Leadership Datamart) ────────────────► Show CRO Cockpit & Download Attestation
4. Navigate: Cryptographic Audit Ledger
   └── Click: Verify Cryptographic Chain ───────────────────► Show green verification banner
5. Navigate: SOC 2 Audit Studio
   └── Quick pan of automated trust criteria report
6. End on High Note: Return to main dashboard showing live Singapore Cloud Run deployment.
```

### 3. Voice & Audio Tips:
* **Microphone:** Use a dedicated headset or USB condenser mic (avoid laptop built-in mic).
* **Pacing:** Speak at a crisp, confident cadence (~140 words per minute).
* **Tone:** Professional, authoritative, and energetic—like an enterprise product lead presenting to a board of banking directors.
* **Timing Cushion:** The script is calibrated for **~2 minutes 45 seconds**, giving you 15 seconds of buffer to stay strictly under the 3-minute limit.
